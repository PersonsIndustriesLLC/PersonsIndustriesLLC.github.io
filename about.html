Who We Are

Persons Industries LLC is an engineering firm built around one idea: mechanical, electrical, and software engineering work better as one integrated discipline than as three separate vendors.

We design, fabricate, and support engineered systems — from CAD models and welded hardware to PCB repair and AI-driven autonomous software. Our team carries a project from initial requirements through build, integration, and test, so nothing gets lost in the handoff between disciplines.

We partner with agricultural operators, defense contractors, and construction technology innovators to design and implement systems that meet demanding performance and safety requirements. Every engagement is grounded in technical precision, direct communication, and a mission-first philosophy that puts operational success first.
